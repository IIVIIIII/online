She is my fear and fear is a bone

2023

“She is my fear and fear is a bone” is an experimental short film by Kylie Park. The filmmaker asked random people to contribute a sentence or two to build a narrative. This film was inspired by the results of that process. I was commissioned to provide music for the film using vocal recordings of the narrative. The only condition was that I must obscure the narrative beyond recognition. The majority of sounds heard in my score are manipulations of the voice recordings I was provided with.
